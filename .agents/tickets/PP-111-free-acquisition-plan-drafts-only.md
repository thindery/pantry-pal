## Problem

Pantry Hub has no listed presence on the usual free launch/SaaS directories and no approved community plan. Discovery currently depends almost entirely on an unoptimized five-URL site. JWA/Pantry Hub standing rules: $0 only, and nothing public without Tylor’s yes.

## Behavior

- Create a tracker (`docs/seo/backlink-tracker.md` or the skill CSV). Log only real submissions. Never invent `link_url` rows.
- Prepare **drafts** (not submissions) for a first free wave once P0 honesty/metadata fixes are live: AlternativeTo (as an alternative to Pantry Check, NoWaste, Grocy, KitchenPal), SaaSHub, Indie Hackers product page, SourceForge, Uneed, Peerlist, Fazier, and similar free-fit directories from the catalog.
- Hold Product Hunt until a real launch moment (honest copy, demo video, 3-week warm-up). Hold Show HN unless there is a genuine technical hook. Skip paid expedites and paid review badges. Do not seed G2/Capterra reviews.
- Draft community notes (Reddit / IH) under a 90/10 useful-answer rule. Leave them in the repo or a desk draft for Tylor; do not post.
- Destination URL should be the homepage or a shipped guide, never sign-in.
