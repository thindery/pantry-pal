# REMY-616: SEO Content Hub - Blog Infrastructure & Strategy

**Status:** To Research  
**Priority:** Medium  
**Created:** 2026-07-17

---

## Goal

Set up blog/content hub for ongoing SEO content marketing.

---

## Why

- Fresh content signals to Google
- Long-tail keyword targeting
- Backlink acquisition potential
- Establishes authority in pantry/inventory space

---

## Content Strategy

### Pillar Pages (High-level, broad):
1. "Complete Guide to Pantry Organization" (3000+ words)
2. "Home Inventory Management for Every Household" (2500+ words)

### Cluster Content (Supporting posts):
- "10 Pantry Organization Ideas for Small Kitchens"
- "How to Do a Kitchen Inventory (Step-by-Step)"
- "Best Practices for Food Storage Rotation"
- "Digital vs Paper Pantry Tracking: Pros & Cons"
- "How to Reduce Food Waste with Smart Inventory"
- "Pantry Staples Checklist for New Homeowners"
- "Meal Planning from Your Pantry Inventory"
- "Prepper's Guide to Long-Term Food Storage"

---

## Blog Setup

- /blog/ index page
- /blog/[slug] post pages
- Category/tag system
- RSS feed
- Author profiles
- Related posts
- Social sharing buttons

---

## SEO Requirements

- Clean URLs: /blog/post-title
- BreadcrumbList schema
- Article schema markup
- Author schema
- Reading time indicator
- Table of contents for long posts
- Internal linking strategy

---

## Content Calendar (first 90 days)

- Week 1: Setup blog infrastructure
- Week 2: Pillar page 1
- Week 3-4: 2 cluster posts
- Continue 2 posts/week

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] Blog infrastructure implemented (/blog/, /blog/[slug])
- [ ] First pillar page published
- [ ] First 2 cluster posts published
- [ ] RSS feed available
- [ ] Social sharing configured
- [ ] Content calendar in place for next 3 months
